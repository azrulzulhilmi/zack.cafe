Project Brief: Zack Cafe Website

Theme Overview
Concept: Green Earth
Colors: Warm Cream, Forest Green, Sage Green, and Dark Green
Fonts: Playfair Display for headings and Lato for body text

Tech Stack

HTML5 (for building the structure and content)

CSS3 (for styling, colors, and layout)

Vanilla JavaScript (optional, only needed if you want a clickable mobile menu)

Core Features

Mobile Responsive Layout (ensures the site looks perfect on both phones and computer screens)

Clean Menu Display (a neat, text based list to showcase your drinks and food)

Embedded Google Map (a simple map insert so customers can easily find your cafe)

Website Sections

Header and Hero Section (a welcoming background photo, your logo, and a "View Menu" button)

Our Menu (categorized lists for Coffee, Tea, and Meals)

About Us (a short and friendly paragraph explaining the cozy vibe of Zack Cafe)

Visit Us (your footer containing opening hours, physical address, the map, and social links)

Build Steps

Step 1: The Skeleton (Write your HTML first to get all your text, headings, and lists onto the page)

Step 2: The Paint (Add your CSS file to bring in your Green Earth colors, set your fonts, and arrange the spacing)

Step 3: The Assets (Insert your cafe photos and paste the location code from Google Maps)

Step 4: The Polish (Test the website on different screen sizes and make final adjustments to ensure everything is clean and readable)
